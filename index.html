Training renewal guide

Recommended refresh intervals for core competencies

Hand hygiene
Renew every 12 months
  • 6 months if high personal care or outbreaks
  • Annual acceptable, 6-monthly more defensible
Infection prevention & control
Renew every 12 months
  • Plus event-based refresh (outbreak, policy change)
  • Do not extend beyond a year
NDIS supporting effective communication
Renew every 2–3 years
  • Earlier if BSP or participant needs change
  • Earlier if communication-related incidents occur
Worker orientation
On commencement + refresh
  • Core refresher every 2 years (or annual light touch)
  • Trigger refresh when policies or systems change
Renewal frequency
12 mo 12 mo 2–3 yr Ongoing
Add event-based refresh rules across all modules. Outbreaks, policy changes, and serious incidents should trigger a review regardless of scheduled interval.
